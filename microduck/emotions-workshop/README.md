# Microduck emotions: the design workshop

How the 14 Microduck emotions were designed for the theater episodes (sound first, motion on the shipped policies,
the beak following the sound), moved here from pollen-robotics/microduck_emotions on 2026-10-07. That repo now holds
only what replays the emotions.

- `../EMOTIONS-BRIEF.md`: the original brief (2026-09-04).
- `NOTES.md`: the running log: what was tried, what failed, Rémi's decisions.
- `reports/<emotion>.md`: one report per emotion (formulas, beat tables, rejected options).

The scripts, candidate sounds and renders are in the tag `workshop-2026-10-07` of
https://github.com/pollen-robotics/microduck_emotions (they ran in the author's Microduck workspace, so their paths
are local ones). Episode 3 itself: `../EPISODE3-BRIEF.md`, `../EPISODE3-HANDOFF-2.md`, `../BINDINGS.md`.
