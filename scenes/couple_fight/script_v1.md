# Couple fight, take 1: "the faithful draft" (Rémi's lines, tightened)

Closest to the draft. Seven lines, about 45 s. The escalation is the classic one: late, Lisa, the hearts, the father.

| # | who | line |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Loretta | [cold] You're home late. [pause] Again. |
| 2 | Husband | [tired] Loretta, please. [sighs] Loretta, please. I come from a very, very long session at work. |
| 3 | Loretta | [sarcastic] Oh. [pause] And I assume you were working... with Lisa. |
| 4 | Husband | [exasperated] Loretta, please. Not this again. [firmly] She is just a co-worker. |
| 5 | Loretta | [mocking] Oh yes? [fast] Then why is she always, always messaging you? [shouting] And all those little hearts I see? |
| 6 | Husband | [shouting] They are emojis, Loretta! Everybody sends emojis! [pause] [bitter] Your father never liked me. [hurt] He called me a clank. |
| 7 | Loretta | [shouting] Oh my god, let it go! [fast] It was sixteen years ago and he didn't mean it! |

Notes: the jump from the hearts to the father is the weak point (why does he bring up the father?). Take 2 fixes that.
