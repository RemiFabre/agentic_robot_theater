# Couple fight, take 3: "Lisa is the printer" (punchy, with a twist)

Eight lines, about 50 s. Same escalation as take 2 but tighter, and the reveal at the end reframes the whole fight:
Lisa is the office printer. She sends hearts when the toner is low. Loretta does not care.

Notes: line 6 skips the "you never listen" beat of take 2, the jump from the hearts straight to the father reads as
panic, which is funnier. Nine lines including the button; still shorter than take 2 because the lines are shorter.
The last two lines are the twist and the button; both are calm, which gives the wobbler a contrast after the shouting.
