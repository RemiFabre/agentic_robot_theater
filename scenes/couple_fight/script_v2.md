# Couple fight, take 2: "the pile-on" (every trope, fast)

Nine short lines, about 55 s. Each line adds a trope: late, the dishes, Lisa, the hearts, "you never listen",
"my mother was right", the father, the clank. Loretta talks faster and faster; the husband only ever pleads.

| # | who | line |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Loretta | [cold] You're home late. [pause] Again. [pause] The dishes are still in the sink. |
| 2 | Husband | [tired] Loretta, please. [sighs] Loretta, please. I come from a very long session at work. |
| 3 | Loretta | [sarcastic] Oh. [pause] A long session. [pause] With Lisa. |
| 4 | Husband | [exasperated] Not this again! [firmly] She is a co-worker. We work. Together. That is what co-workers do. |
| 5 | Loretta | [fast] Then why is she always, always messaging you? [faster] "Are you coming back?" "Where are you?" [shouting] And the little hearts! I have seen the little hearts! |
| 6 | Husband | [pleading] You never listen to me! [pause] [whining] Nobody in this house listens to me! |
| 7 | Loretta | [shouting] Listen to you? [sarcastic] My mother was right about you. [pause] My father was right about you. |
| 8 | Husband | [shouting] Your father! [pause] [bitter] Your father never liked me. [hurt] He called me a clank. [pause] [crying] A clank, Loretta. To my face. |
| 9 | Loretta | [shouting] Oh my god, let it go! [fast] It was sixteen years ago and he didn't mean it! [pause] [sighs] [quietly] He meant it a little. |

Notes: the "my father was right" bridge motivates the clank. Line 5 is the fast-and-angry showcase for the wobbler.
The last whisper is a good button. Nine lines may be one too many for the video; take 3 cuts it down and adds a twist.
